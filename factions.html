Factions & Alignment

Every choice you make in the story pushes you toward Order or Chaos. Your alignment, your tier and your path decide which factions will take you.

Order vs Chaos

Like Raava and Vaatu, two forces pull at Republic City.

Two Separate Meters

Order and Chaos are tracked separately. Earning one never takes points away from the other.

Earned Through Choices

Quests give you choices: report the bribe or use it as leverage, hand over the evidence or pocket it.

On Your Record

Your dossier shows whether you lean Order, Chaos or Neutral, with both totals.

Coming soon: the first Order and Chaos choices arrive in Arc 1, Part 2. Factions open after that.
