Bending · Earthbending

Earth Line

Earth Line
Path
Earthbending
Tier
Tier II (50 QP)
Type
Move

Send a steerable ridge of earth along the ground at a target.

How to Use

  • Run /b help EarthLine in game for the controls.
  • Bind it. /b bind EarthLine <slot> puts it on a hotbar slot (1–9).

How to Get It

Foreman Akatsuchi teaches it himself at the end of Lost in Translation: his own trick for laying out a street.
