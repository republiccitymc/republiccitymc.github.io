Bending & Tiers

Every path starts at Tier I and climbs to Tier ★. You climb by earning Quest Points and passing your trainer's exam. Each tier keeps everything before it.

Paths

EarthbendingMetal or Lava WaterbendingIce, Healing and Plants FirebendingLightning or Combustion Chi BlockingEqualist or Kyoshi Warrior Weapons MasterAny blade, no bending

You pick your path on your Registration Form. Changing it later takes forged papers from Shady Zhen, and starts you over at Tier I. Each path's page lists its moves at every tier, its trainers and the roleplay ranks open to it.

The Four Tiers

TierTo unlockWhat you get
I · FormsWhere everyone startsYour starting moves, already on your hotbar
II · Street50 QP + your trainer's exam coming soonCrowd control, defense and your first combos
III · Master250 QP + a boss fightMaster moves, and you choose one path
★ · Legend500 QP + a duel with a legend, then a featYour path's signature move

Quest Points (QP) are your service record. You earn them from the main story (about 41 in Arc 1) and side quests (about 14 more). They're never spent, traded or sold.

Every Tier Hits Harder

On top of new moves, each tier boosts the moves you already have:

TierDamageCooldownsRangeCharge time
I————
II+10%−5%+5%−5%
III+20%−10%+10%−10%
★+30%−15%+15%−15%

Speed, height, knockback and area never change, so a higher tier is an edge, not a one-shot.

Empty Hands

Until Tier III, bending takes an empty main hand: holding anything stops the move. Keep your quest compass in your off hand. From Tier III you can bend while holding things. Weapons Masters always fight with a weapon in hand.

Discipline

Your dossier shows your Discipline under your element: your Tier III path, or the bending styles your tier has opened (Sand for earthbenders, Ice · Healing · Plant for waterbenders, both from Tier II).

Technique Scrolls

Rare boss drops that teach one move early, for good. Right-click to read one. The wrong path can't read it, but can sell it. The first is Scroll: Catapult from Pitmaster Jax, for earthbenders.
