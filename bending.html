Bending & Tiers

Every path starts at Tier I. Your tier goes up automatically as you earn Quest Points, and each new tier unlocks your trainer's quests, which teach you that tier's moves. You keep everything you learn.

At a glance
Tier up
Automatic: Tier II at 50 QP, Tier III at 250 QP, Tier ★ at 500 QP.
New moves
Your trainer's quests unlock the moment you reach a tier.
Subelements
Benders only: one at Tier II, two at Tier III, all three at Tier ★. You choose the order.
Empty hands
Until Tier III, bend with nothing in your main hand (the compass can go in your off hand).

How Tiers Work

Earn Quest PointsFrom the main story and side quests.
Tier up automaticallyNo exam, no fight: you rank up the moment you hit the QP.
Your trainer's quests unlockFinish them to learn your new moves.
TierUnlocks atTrainers
I · FormsWhere everyone startsNone: your starting moves are already on your hotbar
II · Street50 QPYoung Lin Beifong (earth), Young Kya (water), Business Man Shinu (fire), Acrobat Ji (chi), Young Bumi (weapons)
III · Master250 QPPenga, Master Kanok, Royal Academy Elite, Ty Lee, Mai
★ · Legend500 QPToph Beifong, Katara, Zuko / Iroh, Suki, Sokka

Quest Points (QP) are your service record. Arc 1 gives about 41 from the main story and about 14 more from side quests. They're never spent, traded or sold.

Subelements

Benders specialize as they rank up: one subelement at Tier II, two at Tier III, all three at Tier ★. You choose the order. Your trainer then sends you to the quests for that subelement's moves.

ElementSubelements
EarthMetalSandLava
WaterIceHealingPlant
FireLightningCombustion

Water: your Tier II pick is Ice or Healing; Plant can come from Tier III. Fire: one at Tier III, the other at Tier ★. Chi blockers and Weapons Masters don't bend, so they have no subelements. Your dossier lists yours as your Discipline.

Every Tier Hits Harder

On top of new moves, each tier boosts the moves you already have:

TierDamageCooldownsRangeCharge time
I————
II+10%−5%+5%−5%
III+20%−10%+10%−10%
★+30%−15%+15%−15%

Speed, height, knockback and area never change, so a higher tier is an edge, not a one-shot.

Technique Scrolls

Rare boss drops that teach one move early, for good. Right-click to read one. The wrong path can't read it, but can sell it. The first is Scroll: Catapult from Pitmaster Jax, for earthbenders. Water's Ice Wave also comes from a boss scroll.
