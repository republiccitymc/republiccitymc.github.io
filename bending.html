Bending

Bending & Tiers

Every bender, chi blocker and Weapons Master starts at Tier I. Your tier goes up automatically as you earn Quest Points, and each new tier unlocks your trainer's quests, which teach you that tier's moves. You keep everything you learn.

At a glance
Tier up
Automatic: Tier II at 50 QP, Tier III at 250 QP, Tier ★ at 500 QP.
New moves
Your trainer's quests unlock the moment you reach a tier.
Subelements
Benders only. Earth: one at Tier II (Metal or Sand), two at Tier III, all three at Tier ★. Water: one at Tier II (Healing, Ice or Plant), two at Tier III, all three at Tier ★. Fire: one at Tier II, the other at Tier III.
Empty hands
Until Tier III, bend with nothing in your main hand (the compass can go in your off hand).

How Tiers Work

Earn Quest PointsFrom the main story and side quests.
Tier up automaticallyNo exam, no fight: you rank up the moment you hit the QP.
Your trainer's quests unlockFinish them to learn your new moves.
TierUnlocks at
I · FormsWhere everyone starts
II · Street50 QP

Each element's trainers are on its own page.

Quest Points (QP) are your service record. Arc 1 gives about 41 from the main story and about 14 more from side quests. They're never spent, traded or sold.

Subelements

Benders specialize as they rank up. Your trainer sends you to the quests for each subelement's moves.

ElementSubelements
EarthMetalSandLava
WaterIceHealingPlant
FireLightningCombustion

Each subelement is taught by its own teacher. Earth: one subelement at Tier II, Metal or Sand, your pick after Foreman Akatsuchi's jobs; a second at Tier III, where Lava opens too; all three at Tier ★. Water: one subelement at Tier II, Healing, Ice or Plant, your pick after Young Kya's first lesson; a second at Tier III; all three at Tier ★. Fire: pick Lightning or Combustion at Tier II and learn the other at Tier III. Chi blockers and Weapons Masters don't bend, so they have no subelements. Your dossier lists yours under Subelement.

Every Tier Hits Harder

On top of new moves, each tier boosts the moves you already have:

TierDamageCooldownsRangeCharge time
I————
II+10%−5%+5%−5%
III+20%−10%+10%−10%
★+30%−15%+15%−15%

Speed, height, knockback and area never change, so a higher tier is an edge, not a one-shot.

Technique Scrolls

Right-click a scroll to learn its move for good, at any tier. The wrong element can't read it, but can sell it. Scrolls drop from bosses and come from quests and vendors; where each new one comes from is still being decided.
