Combat & Growth

Bending Progression

Bending power is earned through the story, not grinding. Collect Quest Points, pass your promotion exam, and your master unlocks the next tier.

How Promotion Works

There are no levels and no stat grinding.

1. Earn Quest Points

Complete quests and official events. QP can't be traded or bought.

2. Take the Exam

Once you have enough QP, your bending master offers you a promotion quest.

3. Unlock the Tier

Pass, and every ability in the next tier unlocks. Your dossier shows your new Mastery rank.

Heads up: Arc 1, Part 1 is worth 17 QP. Promotion exams open as more of the story goes live.
