Healing Waters

Healing Waters
Path
Waterbending
Subelement
Healing
Tier
Tier II (50 QP)
Type
Move

Heal yourself or someone else with water.

How to Use

  • Yourself. Hold sneak while standing in water or carrying water bottles.
  • Someone else. Sneak and right-click them, and keep looking at them.
  • Bind it. /b bind HealingWaters <slot> puts it on a hotbar slot (1–9).

How to Get It

Young Kya's second quest, Great-Gran's Necklace, after Lost in the Sewers.
