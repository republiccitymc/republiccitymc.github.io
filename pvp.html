Want a real fight? The PvP arenas are free-for-all battlegrounds outside the story. Bring your bending and nothing else, and nobody loses anything.

Contents

Getting In

Walk through the arch at the Sparring Grounds or type /pvp. Both take you to the arena hub, where a portal leads to each arena and one leads back to Republic City.

The Arenas

Three floating arenas, built after famous places from the world of Avatar:

  • Northern Water Tribe
  • Wan Shi Tong's Library
  • Sun Warriors

There are no walls at the edge. Falling off is part of the fight. Arenas repair themselves once everyone has left.

Rules

  • Everyone fights everyone. There are no teams.
  • No gear. Your inventory stays behind and comes back when you leave. Everyone bends at Tier ★ strength in the arenas. Weapons Masters get a training staff, an iron sword, and a rope dart if they've learned Rope Dart.
  • Fall below the arena and you're out. The last player to hit you gets the kill.
  • No rewards. Normal fights give no Quest Points, Yuans, Order or Chaos. Staff run events now and then with prizes.
  • Leaving: take the portal back, or type /leave if nobody has hit you in the last 10 seconds.
