Chi Blocking

Chi Blocking
Type
Non-bender
Style
Speed and pressure points: shut a bender down
Trainers
Acrobat Ji · Ty Lee · Suki
Ranks
Kyoshi Warriors, The Ungiven

Chi blockers don't bend. They strike the body's chi paths to stop a bender cold, and they're fast enough to get there first.

Ty Lee made the art famous. In 130 AG more non-benders are learning it, and not all of them agree on why.

Roleplay Ranks

  • Kyoshi Warriors (Order): chi blockers and weapons masters only. Tier II and 50 Order.
  • The Ungiven (Chaos): chi blockers and weapons masters only. Tier II.
  • Plus the ranks open to every path. See Roleplay Ranks.

Tips

  • Acrobat Stance and High Jump make you hard to pin down: stay mobile and pick your moment.
  • Bola (Tier II) wraps a target and slows them, which sets up everything else.
