Rope Dart

Rope Dart
Path
Weapons Master
Tier
Tier II (50 QP)
Type
Move

Hook the first enemy in your path and yank them to you.

How to Use

  • Left-click with the rope dart in hand. Bumi gives you one.
  • Bind it. /b bind RopeDart <slot> puts it on a hotbar slot (1–9).

How to Get It

Young Bumi's quest Recruitment Day, after Uncle Sokka's Drills.
