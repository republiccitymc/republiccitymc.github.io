Earth Dome

Earth Dome
Path
Earthbending
Tier
Tier II (50 QP)
Type
Combo

Close yourself, or someone else, inside a dome of earth.

How to Use

  • Yourself. Raise Earth (right-click), then Shockwave (right-click).
  • Someone else. Raise Earth (right-click), then Shockwave (left-click).

A combo: you perform it by chaining the moves above. /b help EarthDome shows the sequence in game.

How to Get It

Taught by Young Lin Beifong's quests once you reach Tier II (50 QP).
