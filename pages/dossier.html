City Hall keeps a dossier on every citizen. Anyone can read it, so it's how people know who you are.

Opening a Dossier

  • Right-click your Registration Form or Citizenship Papers to open your own.
  • Crouch and right-click another player, or type /dossier <name>. They have to be online.
  • On your own dossier, the top corners open your Quest Log (/quests) and Travel Map (/wp).

What It Shows

EntryWhat it means
Name and titleYour character's name (username in brackets) and the title you wear.
Service RecordYour Quest Points.
Legal StatusUnregistered, Registration Filed, or Registered Citizen. Click it for achievements.
Place of OriginFrom your Registration Form.
BendingYour path, your Discipline (Tier III path, or the styles your tier has opened) and your Mastery (I, II, III or ★). See Bending & Tiers.
Roleplay RankYour group, or Unaffiliated. See Roleplay Ranks.
Civic AlignmentYour Order and Chaos totals. See Order & Chaos.
PurseYour Yuans.

Achievements and Titles

Each achievement unlocks a title. Wear one at a time with /titles; it shows on your dossier. The Arc 1 achievements are listed on the Main Story page.
