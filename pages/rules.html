City Ordinances

RuleWhat it means
Bend freely, build nothingBend anywhere in the city. You can't place or break blocks, and bent earth goes back on its own.
Doors are privateYou can't open doors or trapdoors, except where a quest sends you.
Bloodbending is a crimeOutlawed in 130 AG. It never unlocks.
Report Triad activityTo Chief Beifong's Metalbending Police. Or don't: see Order & Chaos.

Your Things Are Safe

  • Quest items can't be dropped. If you die, they go to your /backpack.
  • Lost your papers or form? They come back the next time you log in.
  • Nothing for sale makes you stronger. If a store ever opens, it's cosmetics only.

Getting Help

Stuck, bugged or need a hand? Use /helpop <message> in game to reach staff, or ask in the Discord.
