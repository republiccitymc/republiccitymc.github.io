Weapons Masters don't bend. Their abilities bind to the hotbar like bending and work with whatever weapon is in hand, using its damage and any special effects it has.

Your first trainer: Young BumiBy Kya's pond. His quests open at Tier II (50 QP).

Sokka proved a sword and a plan can stand next to the Avatar. Republic City has plenty of people who'd like to prove it again.

Weapon Styles

The weapon in your hand changes how your moves play. Switch weapons and the action bar shows your style. Weapons with no style play the normal way.

StyleWeaponsWhat changes
JianSimple Jian, Bumi's Black JianParry recovers faster, and your next hit after a parry is a Riposte for extra damage.
DaoSimple DaoSweeping Strike cuts twice. Hold a dao in each hand and the second cut hits harder.
MaulSimple Maul, axesLeaping Strike lands with a shockwave that knocks people down for a moment.
HookSimple Gaff HookWeapon Throw becomes a grappling hook: hit a wall to pull yourself to it, or hit an enemy to drag them in.
StaffBasic Training StaffSweeping Strike spins all the way around you, a little weaker.
BowStandard BowWeapon Throw fires a pinning arrow that holds the target in place. Uses one arrow.

Weapons

Any weapon works, but better blades hit harder. Weapons Vendor TenTen at the Sparring Grounds sells blades, and only Weapons Masters can buy them. You can also craft your own from blueprints (TenTen sells most of them). Weapon manuals teach extra moves the way scrolls do for benders: TenTen sells War Cry and Ging's Antiques sells Sidestep. Anyone can buy one, but only a Weapons Master can learn from it. Recipes and stats: Crafting and the Field Guide.
