Weapons Masters don't bend. Their abilities bind to the hotbar like bending and work with whatever weapon is in hand, using its damage and any special effects it has.

Sokka proved a sword and a plan can stand next to the Avatar. Republic City has plenty of people who'd like to prove it again.

Weapons

Any weapon works, but better blades hit harder. Weapons Vendor TenTen at the Sparring Grounds sells blades, and only Weapons Masters can buy them. You can also craft your own from blueprints (TenTen sells most of them). Recipes and stats: Crafting and the Field Guide.
