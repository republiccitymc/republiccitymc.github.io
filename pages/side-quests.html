Side quest walkthroughs (spoilers)

Scrap Lessons

Old Man Shen

  • Open Shen's shop and click Ask about crafting. He gives you 12 bamboo and teaches the basic recipes.
  • Craft a Training Staff at any public crafting table to finish.

Tora

Lady Shijimi

  • Her cat-owl Tora is running laps around Ging's Antiques. Your compass points the way; she appears as you get close.
  • Catch up (she stops to groom) and right-click her to pick her up. Expect claws.
  • Bring her back to Lady Shijimi. Lost her? Ask Shijimi again.
