Short, optional jobs around the Civic District, each worth Quest Points. Look for a floating ! over the person who needs help.

You can run several at once alongside the main story. /quests (or the Quest Log button on your dossier) lists them all with their rewards; click one to make your quest compass follow it.

Walkthroughs

Side quest walkthroughs (spoilers)

Scrap Lessons

Old Man Shen

  • Open Shen's shop and click Ask about crafting. He gives you 12 bamboo and teaches the basic recipes.
  • Craft a Training Staff at any public crafting table to finish.

Tora

Lady Shijimi

  • Her cat-owl Tora is running laps around Ging's Antiques. Your compass points the way; she appears as you get close.
  • Catch up (she stops to groom) and right-click her to pick her up. Expect claws.
  • Bring her back to Lady Shijimi. Lost her? Ask Shijimi again.

The Gutsy Earthbender

Author Jira

  • The wind scattered six pages of Jira's novel. Each one floats and glints, and only you can see it.
  • They're at City Hall, the Hawk Post, the Sparring Grounds, Public Works, Raku's ramen truck and the Ember Fountain. Right-click near each to grab it.
  • Bring all six back to Jira for a first edition signed to you. Other people in town will have heard.

Special Delivery

Postmaster Kotetsu

  • Kotetsu's runner quit to string telegraph wire. Hand-deliver four letters: Mei-Lin, Shopkeeper Jian, Noodle Man Raku and Gatekeeper Ren.
  • Raku runs a shop, so hold his letter and right-click him.
  • Ren's envelope is thick and unmarked. Break the seal (right-click it) or deliver it sealed. People remember which you did.
  • Report back to Kotetsu. His hawk Poshi has hints, and opinions.
