Bending

CommandWhat it does
/b displayLists every ability you can use right now.
/b help <ability>Explains an ability and how to use it.
/b bind <ability> <slot>Puts an ability on a hotbar slot (1–9).
/b toggleTurns your bending off and on.

Your Dossier

CommandWhat it does
/dossier [name]Your official record, or someone else's. Crouch + right-click a player also works. See Dossier & Titles.
/questsYour quest log: main story and side quests, rewards, and which one your compass follows.
/achievements [name]Achievements earned.
/titlesPick the title you wear.
/balYour Yuans.

Getting Around

CommandWhat it does
/wpFast travel to any waypoint you've discovered.
/backpackQuest items that didn't fit, or that you had when you died.
/apartmentGo home, or step back outside. More in Housing.
