Join a group and climb its ranks. Every rank is earned through a quest, offered once you meet its requirements. Your rank shows on your nametag and your dossier.

Coming after Arc 1. Right now staff can give White Lotus, Red Lotus and Republic City Police. The rest, and the quests below, are the plan and may still change.

How Joining Works

Meet the requirementsA tier (from Quest Points), plus Order or Chaos for most groups.
Get the callThe group's recruiter offers you a quest. A pop-up will tell you to come see them.
Finish the questThe reward is the rank. Promotions work the same way, from the same recruiter.

You hold one roleplay rank at a time. Requirements are checked when the quest is offered; Order and Chaos are never taken away for joining.
