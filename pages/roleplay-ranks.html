Join a group and climb its ranks. Every rank is earned through a quest, offered once you meet its requirements. Your rank shows on your nametag and your dossier.

Open now: Republic City Police Cadet, Cabbage Corp Cart Vendor and The Ungiven Member. Coming soon: Enforcer in the three street gangs, once you finish Arc 1. Everything past the first rank comes in later arcs, and the plan may still change.

How Joining Works

Meet the requirementsA tier (from Quest Points), plus Order or Chaos for most groups.
Get the callA message tells you which recruiter wants to see you. They offer you a quest.
Finish the questThe reward is the rank. Promotions work the same way, from the same recruiter.

You hold one roleplay rank at a time. Requirements are checked when the quest is offered; Order and Chaos are never taken away for joining. Every first rank pays 30 Yuans when you earn it. Want to switch groups? Resign by talking to your recruiter. You can rejoin that group later for free, without redoing its quest.

Street gangs. The Terra Triad, the Agni Kai and the Red Monsoon are three separate gangs. Each takes benders of its own element, plus chi blockers and Weapons Masters from its nation (the hometown on your registration form): the Terra Triad takes earthbenders and Earth Kingdom folk, the Agni Kai firebenders and Fire Nation folk, and the Red Monsoon waterbenders and Water Tribe folk (Foggy Swamp included). Nonbenders born in Republic City can't join a gang. They start recruiting once you finish Arc 1. When a later story goes after your own gang, people will know you run with them: you can betray the gang (you lose the rank and gain Order) or cover for them (you keep it and gain Chaos).
