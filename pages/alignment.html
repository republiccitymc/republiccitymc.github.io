Like Raava and Vaatu, two forces pull at Republic City. Your choices earn Order or Chaos: two separate meters, so earning one never costs you the other.

At a glance
Two meters
Order and Chaos add up separately; earning one never lowers the other.
Big choices
Quest 7 (+5) and the Arc 1 finale (+10).
Fights
Spare or arrest a beaten enemy: +1 Order. Finish him: +1 Chaos. First 5 per group.
Why
It decides which Roleplay Ranks will have you.

How It Works

Order

Play it straight
  • Report, cooperate, hand over evidence
  • Spare or arrest a beaten enemy

Chaos

Use leverage
  • Threats, bribes, keeping what you find
  • Finish off a beaten enemy

Your registration shows which way you lean, with both totals.

In Fights

Terra Triad members and the police recruits at the Sparring Grounds don't fight to the death. Beat one down and he gives up and kneels. Then it's your call:

YouWhat happens
Leave him beAfter 30 seconds he gets up and walks off: +1 Order.
Arrest him (Police only)Right-click him with the RCPD Handcuffs you get with the Cadet rank: +1 Order, his wallet and a few extra Yuans. Recruits can't be cuffed.
Finish him off+1 Chaos.

Only your first 5 of each kind count, per group (Terra Triad, Police Recruits). Sparing or arresting counts for quests the same as a kill. The last Triad standing sometimes runs for it instead; catch him for a fatter wallet.

Why It Matters

Alignment decides which roleplay ranks will have you, and how the city's people treat you in later arcs.
