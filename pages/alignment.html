In Fights

Terra Triad members and the police recruits at the Sparring Grounds don't fight to the death. Beat one down and he gives up and kneels. Then it's your call:

YouWhat happens
Leave him beAfter 30 seconds he gets up and walks off: +1 Order.
Arrest him (Police only)Right-click him with your RCPD Handcuffs: +1 Order, his wallet and a few extra Yuans. Recruits can't be cuffed.
Finish him off+1 Chaos.

Only your first 5 of each kind count, per group (Terra Triad, Police Recruits). Sparing or arresting counts for quests the same as a kill. The last Triad standing sometimes runs for it instead; catch him for a fatter wallet.

Why It Matters

Alignment decides which roleplay ranks will have you, and how the city's people treat you in later arcs.
