Learning the Basics

Ask Old Man Shen (market by the Sparring Grounds) about crafting. That starts the Scrap Lessons side quest: he gives you 12 bamboo and teaches the basic recipes.

  • Bamboo Bundle
  • Scrap Steel Ingot
  • Training Staff
  • Arrows, Bow and Shield
  • Concealed Dagger
  • Smoke Bomb

Blueprints

Better gear comes from blueprints. Right-click one to learn its recipe for good; the blueprint is used up, and its description shows the recipe.
