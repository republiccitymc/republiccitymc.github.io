Your First Hour

  1. Talk to Registrar Huan behind the brass grille. He hands you a blank Citizen Registration Form.
  2. Create your character. Right-click the form: type your character's name in chat (one name, or first and last), pick your Place of Origin and your path, then click Sign & File. Choose carefully: changing your path later means forged papers from Shady Zhen, and a name change costs 100 Yuans at City Hall.
  3. Check your abilities. Your starting moves are already on your hotbar. /b display lists them; see Commands for the rest. Bend with an empty hand (put your compass in your off hand); from Tier III you can bend while holding things.
  4. Collect your starter kit from Huan: a training staff, cabbage cookies and the Welcome to Republic City pamphlet. Waterbenders also get water bottles.
  5. Follow the story. Huan sends you to your first job. The Main Story page has walkthroughs if you get stuck.

Finding Your Way

  • Quest compass. Hold it to see your current task above the hotbar. It points along the streets, not through walls.
  • Markers. A floating ! over someone means they have a job for you; ? means go back and report.
  • Waypoints. Landmarks unlock as you walk past them. /wp takes you back to any you've found.
  • Objectives and rewards. When you finish a step, it flashes on screen along with your next one. Quest Points, Yuans and Order or Chaos show on screen when you earn them.
