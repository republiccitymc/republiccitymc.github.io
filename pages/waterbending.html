Waterbenders shape water, ice and the life in plants. They're versatile: attack, defense, movement and healing all come from the same element.

You need water to bend water. You start with a few water bottles, and fountains, canals and the bay are all fair game.

Paths

Waterbending has no Tier III path yet: every waterbender walks the ice line above. Healer and plant paths will come once there are well-made moves for them.

Bloodbending is outlawed in 130 AG and never unlocks.

Tips

  • Keep your water bottles: they're your source anywhere dry.
  • Water Spout and Surge carry you across deep water; Water Bubble keeps the water off you underneath it.
  • You're 25% stronger at night.
