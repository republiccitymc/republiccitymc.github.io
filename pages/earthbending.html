Earthbenders move rock and soil. In 130 AG the city is built by them: Public Works hires earthbenders, and Chief Toph Beifong's Metalbending Police keep its streets.

The purer the earth, the earlier you can bend it. Sand, metal and lava each need their subelement.

Earth that's too impure for you won't move, and you'll see "This earth is too impure for you to bend yet." Bent earth always settles back where it was.

Subelements

No pick: you can learn all three, each from its own teacher. Metal (Young Lin Beifong) and Sand from Tier II, Lava from Tier III. Each subelement's page lists what it bends and its moves.

Tips

  • Scroll: Catapult from Pitmaster Jax teaches Catapult before Tier II.
  • Earth Grab and Earth Dome come from scrolls TenTen sells at the Sparring Grounds: see Technique Scrolls.
  • Earth Armor and Raise Earth need earth nearby: fight near stone and soil, not on brick.
  • Tremorsense (Tier II) lets you feel the ground around you: handy in the dark sewers.
