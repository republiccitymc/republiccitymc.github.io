Earthbenders move rock and soil. In 130 AG the city is built by them: Public Works hires earthbenders, and Chief Toph Beifong's Metalbending Police keep its streets.

The purer the earth, the earlier you can bend it. Metal and lava come only with their Tier III paths.

Earth that's too impure for you won't move, and you'll see "This earth is too impure for you to bend yet." Bent earth always settles back where it was.

Paths

At Tier III, choose one. It's permanent.

Metal

Toph Beifong's own invention, and the badge of her police force. Metalbenders sense the bits of earth left in refined metal.

  • Tier III: Metal Clips, Extraction
  • ★: Metal Cables coming later
  • Bends: iron, gold and copper, ore and block

Lava

Rare and feared. Lavabenders melt earth into magma and turn it back to stone.

  • Tier III: Magma Shot, Lava Manipulation
  • ★: Lava Flow, earned by a feat

  • Scroll: Catapult from Pitmaster Jax teaches Catapult before Tier II.
