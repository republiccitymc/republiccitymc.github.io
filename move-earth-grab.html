Bending · Earthbending

Earth Grab

Earth Grab
Path
Earthbending
Learned from
Scroll: Earth Grab
Price
60 Yuans, Weapons Vendor TenTen
Tier
Any
Type
Move

Root a target to the spot, or drag items lying on earth over to you.

How to Use

  • Grab. Left-click toward the target.
  • Drag. Sneak to pull nearby items to you.
  • Escape. If you're caught, right-click the trap a few times, or get moved by another earth move.
  • Bind it. /b bind EarthGrab <slot> puts it on a hotbar slot (1–9).

How to Get It

Buy Scroll: Earth Grab from Weapons Vendor TenTen at the Sparring Grounds for 60 Yuans (earthbenders only). Right-click it to learn the move for good, at any tier.
