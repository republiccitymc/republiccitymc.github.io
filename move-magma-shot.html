Magma Shot

Magma Shot
Path
Earthbending
Subelement
Lava
Tier
Tier III (250 QP)
Type
Move

Fire balls of magma, up to three in a row.

How to Use

  • Run /b help MagmaShot in game for the controls.
  • Bind it. /b bind MagmaShot <slot> puts it on a hotbar slot (1–9).

How to Get It

Learned through a trainer quest once you have Lava. Penga points you to a lavabender once you reach Tier III.
