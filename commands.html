Bending

CommandWhat it does
/b displayLists every ability you can use right now.
/b help <ability>Explains an ability and how to use it.
/b bind <ability> <slot>Puts an ability on a hotbar slot (1–9).
/b toggleTurns your bending off and on.

Your Registration

CommandWhat it does
/reg [name]Your registration, or someone else's (/registration works too). Crouch + right-click a player also works. See Registration & Achievements.
/questsYour quest log: main story, your training, Roleplay Rank quests and side quests, rewards, and which one your compass follows.
/guideReread any Civic Pamphlet you've seen. Clerk Huan hands out paper copies of all of them for free.
/achievements [name]Achievements earned. Pick the title you wear here too.
/balYour Yuans.

Getting Around

CommandWhat it does
/wpFast travel to any waypoint you've discovered.
/backpackQuest items that didn't fit, or that you had when you died.
/apartmentGo home, or step back outside. More in Housing.

Talking to People

CommandWhat it does
/msg <name> <text>Private message. /r replies.
/mailLeave a letter for someone who's offline.
/helpop <text>Reach city staff.
/rulesThe server rules.
