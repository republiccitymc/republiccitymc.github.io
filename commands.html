Quick Reference

Commands

The handful of commands you'll actually use.

Citizen Records

CommandWhat it does
/dossier <name>Open a citizen's records: title, QP, origin, bending, faction, alignment and residence. The player must be online.
/profile <name>Same as /dossier.
Crouch + right-click a playerOpen their dossier. Your main hand must be empty.
Right-click your form or papersOpen your own dossier.
/achievements <name>See a citizen's achievements. You can also click Legal Status on any dossier.
/titlesChoose which earned title shows on your dossier. You can wear one at a time.

Bending

CommandWhat it does
/b bind <ability> [slot]Bind an ability to a hotbar slot (1–9). Leave out the slot to use the one you're holding.
/b displayList the abilities you can use at your current tier.
/b help <ability>Explain how an ability works and how to use it.
/b clear [slot]Clear one bound slot, or all of them.

Inventory

CommandWhat it does
/backpackYour quest items. Anything that can't fit in your inventory, or would have dropped on death, ends up here.
