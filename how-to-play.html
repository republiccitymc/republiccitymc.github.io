Your First Hour

  1. Create your character. The screens open as soon as you join: pick your Place of Origin and your bending, then sign. Choose carefully: changing your path later means forged papers from Shady Zhen.
  2. HTalk to Clerk Huan Inside City Hall. He starts Arc 1 and hands you a starter kit: a training staff, Running Shoes (twice as fast), cabbage cookies and the Welcome to Republic City pamphlet. Waterbenders also get water bottles.
  3. Check your moves. They're already on your hotbar; /b display lists them. Bend with an empty hand (compass in your off hand) until Tier III.
  4. Follow your quest compass. Hold it to see your task; it points along the streets. Stuck? The Arc 1 page has walkthroughs.
  5. Walk past landmarks. Each one you pass becomes a fast-travel point on /wp.
  6. Open your registration. /reg shows your tier, Quest Points, Yuans and quest log.

Finding Your Way

  • Quest compass. Hold it to see your current task above the hotbar. It points along the streets, not through walls.
  • Markers. A floating ! over someone means they have a job for you; ? means go back and report.
  • Waypoints. Landmarks unlock as you walk past them. /wp takes you back to any you've found.
  • Objectives and rewards. When you finish a step, it flashes on screen along with your next one. Quest Points, Yuans and Order or Chaos show on screen when you earn them.

Rules

  • Bend freely, build nothing. Bend anywhere in the city. You can't place or break blocks, and bent earth goes back on its own.

Your Items Are Safe

  • Quest items can't be dropped. If you die, they go to your /backpack.
  • Lost your papers? They come back the next time you log in.

Getting Help

Stuck, bugged or need a hand? Use /helpop <message> in game to reach staff, or ask in the Discord.
