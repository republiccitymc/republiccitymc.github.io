Getting Started

You arrive in the City Hall lobby with no papers.

Talk to Registrar Huan

He's behind the brass grille. Right-click him and he hands you a blank Citizen Registration Form.

Fill out your form

Right-click the form in your hand. Pick your Place of Origin, then your Path, then click Sign & File. This is permanent; only staff can reset it.

Check your abilities

Your starting abilities are bound to your hotbar automatically. /b display lists what you can use, /b help <ability> explains one, and /b bind <ability> <slot> moves it.

Grab your starter kit

Talk to Huan again: a training staff and cabbage cookies (Cabbage Corp keeps sending them). Waterbenders also get water bottles.

Follow the story

The line above your hotbar shows your current task, and your quest compass points the way along the streets. A floating ! means someone has a job for you; ? means report back. Quest items you can't carry go to /backpack.

Your records

Right-click your form or papers to open your dossier, or look anyone up with /dossier <name> (or crouch + right-click them). Earn achievements, then wear a title with /titles.

Get around

Important places unlock as waypoints when you walk by. Type /wp to fast travel back.

Origins

Earth Kingdom: Ba Sing Se, Omashu, Gaoling, Kyoshi Island, Chin Village
Fire Nation: Capital City, Ember Island, Shu Jing
Water Tribes: Northern, Southern, Foggy Swamp
United Republic: Republic City

Paths

Waterbender, Earthbender, Firebender, or two non-bender paths: Chi Blocker and Weapons Master. Any origin can take any path. No player airbenders.

Rules

The full server rules are pinned in #rules on our Discord. In game, the city works like this:

Bend Freely, Build Nothing

Bend anywhere in the city, but you can't place or break blocks. Doors and trapdoors still work.

Quest Items Are Safe

They can't be dropped. If you die, they go to your /backpack.

No Bloodbending

It's outlawed in 130 AG and never unlocks.
