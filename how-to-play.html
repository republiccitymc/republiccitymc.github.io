Set Up Minecraft 1.21.11

Do this once. After that, just pick the installation and press Play.

Open the Minecraft Launcher

Select Minecraft: Java Edition in the left sidebar.

Make a new installation

Click the Installations tab at the top, then New installation. Name it Republic City.

Pick the version

Open the Version dropdown, choose release 1.21.11, then click Create.

Launch it

Go back to the Play tab, pick Republic City in the dropdown next to the green Play button, and click Play. The first launch downloads the version and takes a minute.

Add the server

In game: Multiplayer → Add Server. Server Name: Republic City MC. Server Address: Address coming soon. Click Done, then double-click the server to join.

Your First Hour

You arrive in the City Hall lobby with no papers.

Talk to Registrar Huan

He's behind the brass grille. Right-click him and he hands you a blank Citizen Registration Form.

Fill out your form

Right-click the form in your hand. Pick your Place of Origin, then your Path, then click Sign & File. Your abilities are bound to your hotbar automatically.

Grab your starter kit

Talk to Huan again: a training staff and cabbage cookies (Cabbage Corp keeps sending them). Waterbenders also get water bottles.

Follow the story

The line above your hotbar shows your current task, and your quest compass points the way along the streets. A floating ! means someone has a job for you; ? means report back.

Your origin and path are permanent once you sign. Only staff can reset them.

Origins

Earth Kingdom: Ba Sing Se, Omashu, Gaoling, Kyoshi Island, Chin Village
Fire Nation: Capital City, Ember Island, Shu Jing
Water Tribes: Northern, Southern, Foggy Swamp
United Republic: Republic City

Paths

Waterbender, Earthbender, Firebender, or two non-bender paths: Chi Blocker and Weapons Master. Any origin can take any path. There are no player airbenders.

Rules

The full server rules are pinned in the #rules channel on our Discord. In game, the city works like this:

Bend Freely, Build Nothing

Bend anywhere in the city, but you can't place or break blocks. Doors and trapdoors still work.

Quest Items Are Safe

They can't be dropped. If you die, they go to your /backpack.

No Bloodbending

It's outlawed in 130 AG and never unlocks.
