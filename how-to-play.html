Your First Steps

  1. Create your character. This opens as soon as you join. Pick your Place of Origin and your path, then sign your papers. Choose carefully: changing your path later means forged papers from Shady Zhen, and a name change costs 100 Yuans at City Hall.
  2. Talk to Registrar Huan inside City Hall to start Arc 1. He also hands you a starter kit: a training staff, cabbage cookies and the Welcome to Republic City pamphlet. Waterbenders also get water bottles.
  3. Check your abilities. Your starting moves are already on your hotbar. /b display lists them; see Commands for the rest. Bend with an empty hand (put your compass in your off hand); from Tier III you can bend while holding things.
  4. Follow your quest compass. It tracks your current quest and points you to the next step (more under Finding Your Way). The Arc 1 page has walkthroughs if you get stuck.

Finding Your Way

  • Quest compass. Hold it to see your current task above the hotbar. It points along the streets, not through walls.
  • Markers. A floating ! over someone means they have a job for you; ? means go back and report.
  • Waypoints. Landmarks unlock as you walk past them. /wp takes you back to any you've found.
  • Objectives and rewards. When you finish a step, it flashes on screen along with your next one. Quest Points, Yuans and Order or Chaos show on screen when you earn them.

Rules

  • Bend freely, build nothing. Bend anywhere in the city. You can't place or break blocks, and bent earth goes back on its own.

Your Items Are Safe

  • Quest items can't be dropped. If you die, they go to your /backpack.
  • Lost your papers? They come back the next time you log in.

Getting Help

Stuck, bugged or need a hand? Use /helpop <message> in game to reach staff, or ask in the Discord.
