Main Story

Arc 1
Title
The Swarm & The Underworld
Quests
9 in three parts, plus a finale
Starts
Registrar Huan, City Hall lobby
Rewards
About 41 QP, 580 Yuans, your own apartment
Choices
Two Order or Chaos decisions

The story starts the moment you walk into City Hall. Spider-Rats in the walls are only the beginning: something is moving under the Civic District, and the Terra Triad is part of it.

Walkthroughs are hidden below; open one only if you're stuck.

The Three Parts

PartQuestsWhat happens
1: The Spider-Rat Crisis1–4Get registered, help the neighborhood with a Spider-Rat problem, and follow it underground.
2: Finding Your Feet5–6Meet the Avatar, learn the Civic District, and prove you can hold your own.
3: The Underworld7–9Follow the trail deeper. That's all we'll say.

Finishing Quest 4 earns the key to your own room at Unity Flats: see Housing. Want more to do along the way? Try the side quests.

Walkthroughs

Part 1: The Spider-Rat Crisis (spoilers)

Quest 1: No Papers, No Problem

City Hall Lobby · Registrar Huan

  • Talk to Registrar Huan. He gives you a blank Registration Form.
  • Right-click the form: your character's name, origin and path, then Sign & File. Talk to Huan again for your starter kit.
  • Huan sends you to Mei-Lin in Unity Flats, Apt 2A.

Quest 2: Pest Control

Unity Flats, Apt 2A · Mei-Lin

  • Kill the 3 Spider-Rats in her bathroom.
  • Right-click the gnawed hole in the baseboard (floating marker).
  • Talk to Mei-Lin. She sends you to Shopkeeper Jian.

Quest 3: Something's Nesting

Jian's Tea Shop · Shopkeeper Jian

  • Talk to Jian, then go through his storage room and down to the cellar.
  • Kill the Spider-Rat Soldier in the cellar. It enrages below 30% health.
  • Report back to Huan.

Quest 4: Into the Depths

City Hall → Public Works alley → Civic Sewers

  • Huan gives you an Emergency Inspection Dispatch. Show it to Gatekeeper Ren in the alley behind Public Works, then walk through the gate behind him. Died or got lost? Walk through the gate again, or use /wp → The Civic Sewers.
  • In the sewers, finish all three in any order: kill 6 Spider-Rats, smash 4 egg sacs, cut 3 webs off Pipe-Fitter Kuo and talk to him.
  • Follow the glowing crystals down to the Spider-Rat Queen's nest and defeat her. Everyone on the quest within 40 blocks gets credit.
  • Bring her fang to Huan: Citizenship Papers, 100 Yuans and the key to your apartment at Unity Flats. He sends you to Avatar Aang.
Part 2: Finding Your Feet (spoilers)

Quest 5: Welcome to the City

City Hall · Avatar Aang

  • Aang sends you on a tour. Visit the four stops in any order: the Library, Public Works, the Sparring Grounds and the Messenger Hawk Post. Each one is also a waypoint.
  • At Public Works you'll see something you shouldn't. Finish the tour, then report back to Aang.

Quest 6: Proving Grounds

Sparring Grounds · Avatar Aang

  • Tell Aang what you saw. He wants proof you can handle yourself first.
  • At the Sparring Grounds, beat one police recruit from each discipline: water, earth, fire and weapons master.
  • Report back to Aang.
Part 3: The Underworld (big spoilers)

Quest 7: A Taste of Suspicion

Public Works alley · Gatekeeper Ren

  • Aang sends you to squeeze Ren. Your first big choice: lean on him the honest way (Order) or with a threat (Chaos).
  • Ren gives up a name and a place. Head to Ging's Antiques.

Quest 8: The Antiques Front

Ging's Antiques · Shopkeeper Ging

  • Get the basement key from Ging and open the door at the back of the shop.
  • Clear the 3 guards, then search the desk in the back office.

Quest 9: Cut the Supply Line

Beneath Ging's Antiques

  • Find and defeat the boss in the depot. Read his warnings: when he raises his foot, jump. Halfway through, his crew joins in.
  • Report to Aang for the Arc 1 finale and one last Order or Chaos choice.
