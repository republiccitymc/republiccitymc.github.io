Quest 1: No Papers, No Problem

City Hall Lobby · Registrar Huan

  • Talk to Registrar Huan in the City Hall lobby. He gives you a blank Registration Form.
  • Right-click the form: choose your origin, then your bending path, then Sign & File.
  • Huan can't process your citizenship while the district is overrun with spider-rats. He sends you to Mei-Lin in Unity Flats.

Quest 2: Pest Control

Unity Flats, Apt 2A · Mei-Lin

  • Kill the 3 Spider-Rats in Mei-Lin's bathroom.
  • Right-click the gnawed hole in the baseboard (look for the floating marker).
  • Talk to Mei-Lin again. She sends you to Shopkeeper Jian, who just ran out of his tea shop screaming.

Quest 3: Something's Nesting

Jian's Tea Shop · Shopkeeper Jian

  • Talk to Jian, then head into the cellar through his backroom.
  • Kill the Spider-Rat Soldier in the cellar. It enrages below 30% health, so finish it fast.
  • Report back to Registrar Huan.

Quest 4: Into the Depths

City Hall → Public Works service alley → Civic Sewers

  • Tell Huan the rats came up through the sewers. He gives you an Emergency Inspection Dispatch.
  • Go to the service alley behind Public Works and show the dispatch to Gatekeeper Ren. He lets you in after a lot of nervous complaining. Achievement: Down the Drain (title: Tunnel Rat).
  • In the sewers, finish all four tasks in any order:
    • Kill 12 Spider-Rats.
    • Smash 4 egg sacs (they grow back after a short while).
    • Cut 3 of the 5 webs pinning Pipe-Fitter Kuo, then talk to him.
    • Kill 2 Spider-Rat Soldiers.
  • When all four are done, the nest goes quiet. Head to the deepest chamber and defeat the Spider-Rat Queen. She lunges and summons four Spider-Rats every 15 seconds, so clear the adds and keep moving. Everyone within 40 blocks gets credit. Achievement: Queen Slayer (title: Nestbane).
  • You're pulled out of the sewers 5 seconds after she dies. Bring her fang to Huan.
  • Reward: your form is stamped into Citizenship Papers, plus 100 yuan. Achievement: Citizen of the Republic (title: Citizen).
  • Huan tells you to report the deliberate sewer breach to Avatar Aang. That's where Part 2 begins.
