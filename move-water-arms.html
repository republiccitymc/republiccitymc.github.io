Bending · Waterbending

Water Arms

Water Arms
Path
Waterbending
Tier
Tier ★ (500 QP)
Type
Move

Two arms of water that punch, grab, pull, grapple and freeze.

How to Use

  • Start. Tap sneak at a water source. Hold sneak and left-click to stop.
  • Use. Left-click to punch, pull, grapple, grab and throw, or fire ice, depending on the arm's mode.
  • Bind it. /b bind WaterArms <slot> puts it on a hotbar slot (1–9).

How to Get It

Taught by Katara's quests once you reach Tier ★ (500 QP).
