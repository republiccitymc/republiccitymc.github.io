Octopus Form

Octopus Form
Path
Waterbending
Tier
Tier II (50 QP)
Type
Move

Eight arms of water around you: block, and whip anyone close.

How to Use

  • Left-click a water source, then hold sneak to form the tentacles. Left-click to whip.
  • Bind it. /b bind OctopusForm <slot> puts it on a hotbar slot (1–9).

How to Get It

Young Kya's first quest, Lost in the Sewers, once you reach Tier II (50 QP).
