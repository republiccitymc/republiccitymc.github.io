Spider-Rat Queen

Spider-Rat Queen
Found in
The Nest, deepest chamber of the Civic Sewers
Health
50
Melee
3
Story
Arc 1, Quest 4: Into the Depths

How the fight works, how to win it, and what drops.

Contents
  1. The Fight
  2. Tips
  3. Drops

The Fight

  • 50 health solo, more with every extra player (up to 125).
  • Lunges to knock you back.
  • Takes turns between Web Spit (slows you hard for 3 seconds), Summon Brood (3 Spider-Rats) and Brood Swap (trades places with one of her rats).
  • At 40% health she flees to her nest and heals a little while her brood guards her.
  • At 20% she goes into a frenzy.

Tips

  • Follow the glowing crystals down through the Civic Sewers to reach her nest.
  • Watch chat for the ⚠ warning before each special move, then step aside.
  • When she flees to her nest at 40%, go after her: she heals while her brood holds you off.
  • Bring friends. She gets more health per player, but everyone who does 10% of the damage gets their own drops.

Drops

Everyone who does at least 10% of the damage gets their own roll.

  • Spider-Rat Queen Fang (quest item, 100%)
  • Spider-Rat Pelt ×2–4 (100%)
  • Spider-Rat Queen Chitin (25%)
