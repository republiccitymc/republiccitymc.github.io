Waterbenders shape water, ice and the life in plants. They're versatile: attack, defense, movement and healing all come from the same element.

You need water to bend water. You start with a few water bottles, and fountains, canals and the bay are all fair game.

What You Can Bend

From tierSources
IWater and snow

Ice needs the Ice subelement, and plants need Plant.

Subelements

One subelement at Tier II: Healing (Young Kya), Ice (Haku) or Plant (Moku), your pick after Kya's first lesson. A second at Tier III, and all three at Tier ★. Each subelement's page lists what it bends and its moves.

Bloodbending is outlawed in 130 AG and never unlocks.

Trainers

TierTrainer
IIYoung KyaKatara's daughter. Her first quest teaches Octopus Form and Water Flow. Then you pick your first subelement: she teaches Healing (Healing Waters) herself, sends you to Haku at Maru's Butchery for Ice, or to Moku by the Public Library for Plant. The others wait for Tier III.
IIIMaster KanokNorthern Tribe diplomat.
★KataraMaster healer.

Tips

  • Keep your water bottles: they're your source anywhere dry.
  • Water Spout and Surge carry you across deep water; Water Bubble keeps the water off you underneath it.
  • You're 25% stronger at night.
