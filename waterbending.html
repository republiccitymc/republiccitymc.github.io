Waterbenders shape water, ice and the life in plants. They're versatile: attack, defense, movement and healing all come from the same element.

K
Your first trainer: Young KyaAt her pond. Her lessons open at Tier II (50 QP).

You need water to bend water. You start with a few water bottles, and fountains, canals and the bay are all fair game.

What You Can Bend

From tierSources
IWater and snow

Ice needs the Ice subelement, and plants need Plant.

Subelements

One subelement at Tier II: Healing (Young Kya), Ice (Iceman Haku) or Plant (Moku), your pick after Kya's first lesson. A second at Tier III, and all three at Tier ★. Each subelement's page lists what it bends and its moves.

Bloodbending is outlawed in 130 AG and never unlocks.

Trainers

TierTrainer
IIKYoung KyaKatara's daughter. Her first quest teaches Octopus Form and Water Flow. Then you pick your first subelement: she teaches Healing (Healing Waters) herself, sends you to Iceman Haku at Maru's Butchery for Ice, or to Plantbender Moku by the Public Library for Plant. The others wait for Tier III.
III, ★Coming in a later arc.

Tips

  • Keep your water bottles: they're your source anywhere dry.
  • Water Spout and Surge carry you across deep water; Water Bubble keeps the water off you underneath it.
  • You're 25% stronger at night.
