Behind the Scenes

The Build

Recreating Republic City in Minecraft, one district at a time.

I'm Hammy. I've built Avatar projects in Minecraft on and off for about ten years, including Wan Shi Tong's Library, the Sun Warrior Ruins and an earlier Republic City, all on Planet Minecraft. When the newest Avatar teasers dropped, I decided to build the city properly: not just as a map, but as a place you can live in.

The style is Art Deco at city scale, with Earth Kingdom, Fire Nation and Water Tribe influences meeting in the streets. Screenshots use Complementary Shaders.
