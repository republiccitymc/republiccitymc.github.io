REPUBLIC CITY MC

An immersive, canon-faithful experience set in 130 AG during the rapid industrialization of Republic City. Train under legendary bending masters, join powerful factions, uncover the city's earliest mysteries, and carve your own legacy in a strictly non-pay-to-win environment.

Join The Discord Read the Wiki

A City Reborn

A narrative-driven RPG woven into the Avatar universe. Growth is earned, not given.

Quest-Point Progression

Advance your bending tiers by earning Quest Points (QP) and proving your mastery through promotion exams.

City Economy & Trade

Earn Yuans through quests and trade, eat at local spots like Kwong's Cuisine, and gear up at the Civic Market.

Challenging PvE

Test your bending in custom combat encounters. Defend the Civic District from sewer vermin, street gangs and hidden threats.

Player Housing

Earn your way off the newcomer waitlist and into your own apartment, starting with Unity Flats in the Civic District.

Strictly Non-P2W

Status and combat power are earned through gameplay. Our EULA-compliant store is cosmetics only.

Deep Roleplay

Canon-faithful roleplay in a young, fast-growing city full of sociopolitical tension.
