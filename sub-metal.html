Toph Beifong's own invention, and the badge of her police force. Metalbenders sense the bits of earth left in refined metal.

What You Can Bend

Iron, gold and copper, ore and block.

Moves

MoveTierWhat it does
  • Metal Clips
Tier IIThrow iron ingots that lock around a target as armor, then move them where you want.
  • Extraction
Tier IIPull the metal out of ore, sometimes two or three times over.

Coming later: Metal Cables.

How to Get It

Pick one of Sand, Metal or Lava at Tier II, a second at Tier III, and the last at Tier ★. You choose the order. Young Lin Beifong teaches it herself.
