Firebenders make their own fire from their breath and chi. No source needed, which makes them the most aggressive path in the city.

In 130 AG industry is booming, and firebenders who can run a boiler or a power line are in demand.

Paths

At Tier III, choose one. It's permanent.

Lightning

Cold-blooded fire. In Republic City, lightning-benders are starting to find work in the power plants.

  • Tier III: Lightning
  • ★: Lightning Redirection coming later

Combustion

Very rare: an explosion focused through the mind. Combustion-benders are feared for good reason.

  • Tier III: Combustion
  • ★: coming later

Roleplay Ranks

  • Agni Kai (Chaos): the firebender gang. Tier I and 25 Chaos to join.
  • Plus the ranks open to every path: police, United Forces, White Lotus, Red Lotus and Cabbage Corp. See Roleplay Ranks.

Tips

  • Fire Jet is your escape and your gap-closer: keep it bound.
  • Heat Control puts out fires on you, and melts ice and snow in your way.
  • You're 25% stronger by day.
