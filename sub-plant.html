What You Can Bend

Plants as a water source: leaves, grass, flowers, vines, moss, cactus, sugar cane, melons and pumpkins.

Moves

MoveTierWhat it does
  • Plant Tether
Tier IIGrow a vine from plants to pull an enemy in or pull yourself.
  • Drain
Tier ★Draw water out of nearby plants to fill bottles or fire blasts.

How to Get It

One subelement at Tier II: Healing, Ice or Plant, your pick after Young Kya's first lesson. A second at Tier III. All three at Tier ★. Pick Plant after Young Kya's first lesson and she sends you to Moku, a Foggy Swamp Tribe plantbender by the Public Library.
