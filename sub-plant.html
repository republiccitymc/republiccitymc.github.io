Plant (Waterbending)

Plant
Element
Waterbending
Subelements
Ice · Healing · Plant
From
Tier II (50 QP)

Drawing the water out of living things, the way the Foggy Swamp Tribe does.

Contents
  1. What You Can Bend
  2. Moves
  3. How to Get It

What You Can Bend

Plants as a water source: leaves, grass, flowers, vines, moss, cactus, sugar cane, melons and pumpkins.

Moves

No moves of its own: it opens up new things to bend with the moves you already know.

How to Get It

Pick Ice or Healing at Tier II. At Tier III take the other one or Plant, and the last at Tier ★. You choose the order. Your water trainer shows you.
