Bending · Weapons Master

War Cry

War Cry
Path
Weapons Master
Learned from
Manual: War Cry
Price
100 Yuans, Weapons Vendor TenTen
Tier
Any
Type
Move

A Water Tribe war cry: you speed up, and enemies around you lose their nerve (weaker and slower for a few seconds).

How to Use

  • Left-click with a weapon.
  • Bind it. /b bind WarCry <slot> puts it on a hotbar slot (1–9).

How to Get It

Buy Scroll: War Cry from Weapons Vendor TenTen at the Sparring Grounds for 100 Yuans. Any path can buy it, but only Weapons Master can learn it. Right-click it to learn the move for good, at any tier.
