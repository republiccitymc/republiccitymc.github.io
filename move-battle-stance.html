Battle Stance

Battle Stance
Path
Weapons Master
Tier
Tier I (Start)
Type
Move

Plant your feet: more strength and resistance, but you move slower.

How to Use

  • Run /b help BattleStance in game for the controls.
  • Bind it. /b bind BattleStance <slot> puts it on a hotbar slot (1–9).

How to Get It

You start with it. It's already on your hotbar when you create your character.
