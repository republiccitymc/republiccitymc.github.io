Every citizen gets a companion: a brown ostrich horse, full size, that follows you around and that you can ride. Train it and it fights beside you. Other looks are cosmetic only, so a paid look is never stronger than the free one.

Contents
  1. Your Companion
  2. Training
  3. Looks

Your Companion

  • Call it or send it home with /pet, or crouch and right-click it. That menu also names it, trains it and changes its look.
  • Ride it by right-clicking your ostrich horse. Hold W to walk, sprint to gallop, jump to clear about 2 blocks. No saddle needed.
  • Name it from the menu: up to 16 letters, shown over its head.
  • If it gets hurt badly, it rests for a minute before you can call it again.

Training

An untrained companion just follows you. Each level costs 1,000 Yuans and is the same for every look. From level 1 it fights whatever you fight, and every level adds health and damage, up to level 5. Out of a fight it heals on its own.

Looks

A look changes how your companion looks, nothing else: training, health and damage stay the same. Buy one from the /pet menu once, keep it for good, and switch any time. Companions and looks can't be traded.
