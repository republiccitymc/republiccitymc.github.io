Hidden Caches

Barrels and chests around the city restock every day at midnight Eastern. A stocked cache glows: green sparkles are common, blue dust rare, purple magic epic.

  • The first person to open one claims it for the day. Take what you want; click the gold Yuan nuggets and Quest Point stars to cash them in.
  • You can claim 3 a day.
  • Inside: Yuans, food, materials, now and then a weapon, Quest Points, or (epic only) a Spider Fang Dagger blueprint or a technique scroll.
