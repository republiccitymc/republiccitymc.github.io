Making a Living

Economy & Housing

Republic City runs on two things: Yuans in your pocket and Quest Points on your record.

Two Currencies

💴

Yuans

The city's everyday money.

Earn from: quests, events and selling items
Spend on: food, gear, crafting materials and housing

📜

Quest Points (QP)

Your service record to the city.

Earn from: quests and official events
Unlocks: bending tiers and faction ranks
Can't be: traded, sold or bought

Housing

Somewhere to store your hard-earned items and make your own.

Your First Apartment

Finishing Arc 1 gets you off the newcomer waitlist and into Unity Flats in the Civic District. Thin walls, loud trolleys, a neighbor who files a lot of complaints.

Moving Up

More homes in other districts will be for sale with Yuans as each district opens.

Strictly Non-Pay-to-Win

Status and power in Republic City are earned by playing. Our EULA-compliant store sells cosmetics only. No QP, no tiers, no gear.
