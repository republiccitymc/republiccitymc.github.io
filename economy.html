Technique scrolls. Right-click one to learn its move for good, at any tier. Anyone can buy any scroll, but only its own path can learn it. Crafting

Public crafting tables stand all over the Civic District, but you have to learn a recipe before you can craft it. Vanilla Minecraft recipes work as normal. Ingredients and effects for every item are in the Field Guide.

Learning the Basics

Ask Old Man Shen (market by the Sparring Grounds) about crafting. That starts the Scrap Lessons side quest: he gives you 12 bamboo and teaches the basic recipes.

  • Bamboo Bundle
  • Scrap Steel Ingot
  • Training Staff
  • Arrows, Bow and Shield
  • Concealed Dagger
  • Smoke Bomb

Blueprints

Better gear comes from blueprints. Right-click one to learn its recipe for good; the blueprint is used up, and its description shows the recipe.

Forged Papers

Regret your path? Shady Zhen can make a new you. Once a week, in his alley:

OptionCostWhat changes
New Bending150 YuansPick new bending. Your bending starts again at Tier I. Everything else stays.
New Identity250 YuansA new name, origin and bending. Your story, Quest Points, Yuans and apartment stay.
A Whole New LifeFreeEverything is wiped and you rejoin as a brand-new citizen.

Hidden Loot Boxes

Barrels and chests around the city restock every day at midnight Eastern. A stocked loot box glows: green sparkles are common, blue dust rare, purple magic epic.

  • The first person to open one claims it for the day. Take what you want; click the gold Yuan nuggets and Quest Point stars to cash them in.
  • You can claim 3 a day.
  • Inside: Yuans, food, materials, now and then a weapon, Quest Points, or (epic only) a Spider Fang Dagger blueprint or a technique scroll.
