How to Fight

  • Watch chat for the ⚠. Before any special move, an enemy warns you in chat (⚠ winds up an Earth Blast!) and pauses. That pause is your chance to step aside, jump or back off.
  • Read the health bar. Hit something and a bar appears by its name, green when it's healthy and red when it's nearly down.
  • ★ Veterans. About one enemy in twenty is a Veteran: a gold name and about 50% more health. Beat one and you get a second wallet roll (or extra pelts from a Spider-Rat Soldier).
  • Punish a mistake. A Bruiser who misses his charge is dazed and takes extra damage for a moment. Earthbending recruits hide behind a stone guard; wait it out.
  • Keep an eye on your loot. After a Triad fight, a pickpocket may run in and grab what's on the ground. Catch him and he drops it.
  • Recruits don't fight to the death. At the Sparring Grounds a beaten recruit yields and gives you a tip about their element. It still counts as a win.
  • Bosses scale with your group. The Spider-Rat Queen and Pitmaster Jax get more health for every extra player, and everyone who did at least 10% of the damage gets their own drops.
