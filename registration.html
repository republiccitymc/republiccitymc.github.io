Registration & Achievements

The Hall of Civic Records keeps a registration on every citizen. Anyone can read it, so it's how people know who you are. Open yours with /registration (or /reg), or right-click your Citizenship Papers.

Contents
  1. Opening a Registration
  2. What It Shows
  3. Achievements and Titles

Opening a Registration

  • Crouch and right-click another player, or type /reg <name>. They have to be online.
  • Your own registration has three buttons: Quest Log (/quests), Travel Map (/wp) and Achievements.

What It Shows

LineWhat it means
Name and titleYour character's name and the title you wear.
Status and originUnregistered, Registration Filed or Citizen, and your place of origin.
BendingYour element and tier (I, II, III or ★), and your subelements. See Bending & Tiers.
Quest Points and YuansYour service record and purse, and how far you are from your next tier.
Roleplay RankYour group and rank, or Unaffiliated, plus your Order and Chaos totals. See Roleplay Ranks and Order & Chaos.

Achievements and Titles

Open /achievements (or the button on your registration) to see what you've earned. Many achievements come with a title: pick the one you wear right there. It shows on your registration and over your head. The Arc 1 achievements are listed on the Arc 1 page.
