Earth Pillars

Earth Pillars
Path
Earthbending
Tier
Tier III (250 QP)
Type
Combo

Pillars burst up under everyone near you and throw them into the air.

How to Use

  • Shockwave (tap sneak), Shockwave (hold sneak), then Catapult (release sneak). Landing from high up on Catapult also works.

A combo: you perform it by chaining the moves above. /b help EarthPillars shows the sequence in game.

How to Get It

It comes with Tier III (250 QP): you get it automatically when you reach the tier. Penga's teacher quests are planned.
