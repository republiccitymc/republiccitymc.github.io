Pitmaster Jax

Pitmaster Jax

Ging's Antiques, the Sub-Sewer Depot
Health
60
Melee
6
Story
Arc 1, Quest 9: Cut the Supply Line

A boss from late in the story. Everything below is a spoiler.

Show the fight (spoilers)

The Fight

  • 60 health solo, more with every extra player (up to 150).
  • Almost impossible to knock back.
  • Takes turns between Earth Blast, Earth Catapult (the ground under you bursts, 3 damage), Rock Volley (thrown rocks) and Seismic Slam (a shockwave; jump to dodge it).
  • At half health his crew joins the fight and he can't be hurt for 6 seconds.
  • At 20% he goes into a Pit Fury.

Tips

  • When he raises his foot, jump: that's Seismic Slam.
  • At half health his crew joins in and he can't be hurt for 6 seconds. Deal with the crew first.
  • Earth Catapult bursts the ground under you. Keep moving.
  • Earthbenders: his Scroll: Catapult (15%) teaches Catapult before Tier II.

Drops

Everyone who does at least 10% of the damage gets their own roll.

  • Terra Triad Chit ×1–2 (100%)
  • wallet: 0–10 Yuans (100%)
  • Terra Triad Vault Seal (25%)
  • Scroll: Catapult (15%)
  • Terra Triad Switchblade (5%)
  • On Quest 9: Marked Syndicate Ring (once)
