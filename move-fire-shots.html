Fire Shots

Fire Shots
Path
Firebending
Tier
Tier II (50 QP)
Type
Move

Summon several small fireballs and fire them one by one.

How to Use

  • Run /b help FireShots in game for the controls.
  • Bind it. /b bind FireShots <slot> puts it on a hotbar slot (1–9).

How to Get It

Business Man Homura's quest Brand Visibility & High-Altitude Marketing, after Corporate Synergy & Property Values.
