Earthbending

Earthbending
Element
Earth
Style
Substance: stand your ground, strike when it counts
Subelements
Metal · Sand · Lava
Trainers
Foreman Akatsuchi · Young Lin Beifong (Metal) · Penga · Toph Beifong
Gang
Terra Triad

Earthbenders move rock and soil. In 130 AG the city is built by them: Public Works hires earthbenders, and Chief Toph Beifong's Metalbending Police keep its streets.

The purer the earth, the earlier you can bend it. Sand, metal and lava each need their subelement.

What You Can Bend

From tierEarth
IDirt, coarse dirt, grass, podzol, mycelium, rooted dirt, dirt paths, gravel, clay, stone, cobblestone, granite, diorite, andesite
IIDeepslate, tuff, calcite, dripstone, mud, packed mud, terracotta
IIIOre veins (coal, lapis, redstone, diamond, emerald), coal blocks, amethyst, netherrack, basalt, blackstone
★Worked stone: stone bricks, smooth and polished stone, the city's paving, ancient debris

Earth that's too impure for you won't move, and you'll see "This earth is too impure for you to bend yet." Bent earth always settles back where it was.

Subelements

No pick: you can learn all three, each from its own teacher. Metal (Young Lin Beifong) and Sand from Tier II, Lava from Tier III. Each subelement's page lists what it bends and its moves.

Tips

  • Scroll: Catapult from Pitmaster Jax teaches Catapult before Tier II.
  • Earth Grab, Earth Dome, Earth Kick, Earth Shard, Mud Surge and several metal and lava moves come from Technique Scrolls: see Technique Scrolls.
  • Earth Armor and Raise Earth need earth nearby: fight near stone and soil, not on brick.
  • Tremorsense (Tier ★) lets you feel the ground around you, even in the dark.
