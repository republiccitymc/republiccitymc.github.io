Side Quests

Short, optional jobs around the Civic District, each worth Quest Points. Look for a floating ! over the person who needs help.

At a glance
In Arc 1
8 side quests
All of them
17 QP and 195 Yuans, plus a few keepsakes

You can run several at once alongside the main story. /quests (or the Quest Log button on your registration) lists them all with their rewards; click one to make your quest compass follow it.

Walkthroughs

Side quest walkthroughs (spoilers)

Scrap Lessons

Old Man Shen

  • Open Shen's shop and click Ask about crafting. He gives you 12 bamboo and teaches the basic recipes.
  • Craft a Training Staff at any public crafting table to finish.

Tora

Lady Shijimi

  • Her cat-owl Tora is running laps around Ging's Antiques. Your compass points the way; she appears as you get close.
  • Catch up (she stops to groom) and right-click her to pick her up. Expect claws.
  • Bring her back to Lady Shijimi. Lost her? Ask Shijimi again.

The Gutsy Earthbender

Author Jira

  • The wind scattered six pages of Jira's novel. Each one floats and glints, and only you can see it.
  • They're at City Hall, the Hawk Post, the Sparring Grounds, Public Works, Raku's ramen truck and the Ember Fountain. Right-click near each to grab it.
  • Bring all six back to Jira for a first edition signed to you. Other people in town will have heard.

Special Delivery

Postmaster Kotetsu

  • Kotetsu's runner quit to string telegraph wire. Hand-deliver four letters: Mei-Lin, Shopkeeper Jian, Noodle Man Raku and Gatekeeper Ren.
  • Raku runs a shop, so hold his letter and right-click him.
  • Ren's envelope is thick and unmarked. Break the seal (right-click it) or deliver it sealed. People remember which you did.
  • Report back to Kotetsu. His hawk Poshi has hints, and opinions.

Rookie on the Beat

Police Officer Izumo

  • A Metalbending Police rookie, one week on the job, and the Terra Triad's grunts have taken over the alley on his beat.
  • They mouth off but won't throw the first punch. Hit one and the whole crew comes for you.
  • Beat 3 of them, then report back to Izumo. The grunts come back, so the alley is always good for a fight.

Scoop!

Reporter Shiho

  • Shiho needs proof of the monster under the Civic District. She lends you the press camera.
  • Photograph three spots: the hole in Mei-Lin's bathroom, Jian's caved-in cellar and the sewer gate behind Public Works. Hold the camera there and right-click.
  • Back at Shiho, you choose the headline: the plain truth or a front-page monster. You keep a copy of the paper, and people around town will have read it.

The Petition

Activist Ayame

  • Public Works only hires earthbenders. Ayame, Raku's daughter, wants fair hiring for nonbenders and needs 5 signatures.
  • Hold the petition in your hand while you talk to people (right-click shopkeepers). Some sign straight away, some say no, and some can be talked round.
  • Take it to Clerk Huan at City Hall to have it filed.

Thinning the Ranks

Agni Kai Yura

  • Yura is an Agni Kai firebender, a long way from his own district. He only talks business with people who've earned 15 Chaos.
  • His boss thinks Beifong's recruits make the Civic District look tough. Yura wants their numbers thinned: slay 6 police recruits at the Sparring Grounds, any discipline.
  • A recruit who yields and walks off doesn't count. Finish them before they go. The recruits remember who does that.
  • Report back to Yura. Officer Izumo and Avatar Aang will hear about it.
