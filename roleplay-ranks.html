Roleplay Ranks

Join a group and climb its ranks. Every rank is earned through a quest, offered once you meet its requirements. Your rank shows on your nametag and your registration.

At a glance
Open now
RCPD Cadet, Cabbage Corp Cart Vendor, The Ungiven Member, and Enforcer in the three street gangs (after Arc 1).
How
Meet a tier plus Order or Chaos, then do the recruiter's quest.
Limit

How Joining Works

Meet the requirementsA tier (from Quest Points), plus Order or Chaos for most groups.
Get the callA message tells you which recruiter wants to see you. They offer you a quest.
Finish the questThe reward is the rank. Promotions work the same way, from the same recruiter.
  • One at a time. You hold one roleplay rank and work on one rank quest at a time.
  • Changed your mind? Tell the recruiter "I want to drop this." and take another quest.
  • Nothing is taken away. Requirements are checked when the quest is offered; joining never costs Order or Chaos.
  • Pay: every first rank pays 30 Yuans.
  • Switching groups: resign by talking to your recruiter. You can rejoin later for free, without redoing the quest.

Street gangs

The Terra Triad, the Agni Kai and the Red Monsoon are three separate gangs. They start recruiting once you finish Arc 1. Each takes its own element's benders, plus chi blockers and Weapons Masters from its nation (the hometown on your registration form):

  • Terra Triad: earthbenders and Earth Kingdom folk.
  • Agni Kai: firebenders and Fire Nation folk.
  • Red Monsoon: waterbenders and Water Tribe folk (Foggy Swamp included).

Nonbenders born in Republic City can't join a gang. When a later story goes after your own gang, you choose: betray it (lose the rank, gain Order) or cover for it (keep it, gain Chaos).
