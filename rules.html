Rules

  • Bend freely, build nothing. Bend anywhere in the city. You can't place or break blocks, and bent earth goes back on its own.

Your Items Are Safe

  • Quest items can't be dropped. If you die, they go to your /backpack.
  • Lost your papers? They come back the next time you log in.

Getting Help

Stuck, bugged or need a hand? Use /helpop <message> in game to reach staff, or ask in the Discord.
