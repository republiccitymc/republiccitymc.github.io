Fire Kick
Path
Firebending
Tier

Type
Combo

A short arc of fire off your foot.

How to Use

  • Fire Blast, Fire Blast, hold sneak, Fire Blast.

A combo: you perform it by chaining the moves above. /b help FireKick shows the sequence in game.

How to Get It

Taught by Business Man Shinu's quests once you reach Tier II (50 QP).
