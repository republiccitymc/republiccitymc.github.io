Bending · Waterbending

Torrent

Torrent
Path
Waterbending
Tier
Tier I (Start)
Type
Move

A whip of water that drags and hurts whatever it hits, or a wave that pushes everyone away.

How to Use

  • Torrent. Left-click a water source and hold sneak to form it, then left-click to send it.
  • Wave. Left-click a water source and hold sneak, then release to push everyone back.
  • Bind it. /b bind Torrent <slot> puts it on a hotbar slot (1–9).

How to Get It

You start with it. It's already on your hotbar when you create your character.
