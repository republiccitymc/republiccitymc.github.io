Bending · Waterbending

Water Bubble

Water Bubble
Path
Waterbending
Tier
Tier I (Start)
Type
Move

Push water back to make a pocket of air underwater.

How to Use

  • Hold sneak near water. Click for a short bubble.
  • Bind it. /b bind WaterBubble <slot> puts it on a hotbar slot (1–9).

How to Get It

You start with it. It's already on your hotbar when you create your character.
