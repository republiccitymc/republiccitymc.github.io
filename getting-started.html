Getting Started

You've just stepped off the ferry with no papers. Here's how to get registered, pick your path and become a citizen of Republic City.

Your First Hour

Follow these steps in order.

Join the server

The server address is posted in our Discord. You arrive in the City Hall lobby.

Talk to Registrar Huan

He's behind the brass grille in the City Hall lobby. Right-click him to talk. He hands you a blank Citizen Registration Form.

Fill out your form

Right-click the form in your hand. Choose your Place of Origin, then your Bending Path, then review and click Sign & File. Huan won't send you anywhere until it's filed.

Start bending

Your element is applied the moment you sign, at Mastery I. Bind your abilities with /b bind <ability> (see Commands).

Follow the story

Help clear the spider-rat infestation. When you finish, Huan stamps your form into Citizenship Papers and you become a registered citizen.

Character Creation

Republic City is a melting pot. Any origin can go with any path.

Choose carefully. Once you click Sign & File, your origin and path are permanent. Only staff can reset them.

Bending Paths

Three bending paths and two non-bender paths. There are no player airbenders.

🌊

Waterbender

Push and pull, like the tides. Healing, ice and water control.

🪨

Earthbender

Strength that waits and listens. Rock, seismic sense and, eventually, metal.

🔥

Firebender

Fire is life, not only destruction. Fast, aggressive, and lightning at the top tiers.

✋

Chi Blocker

Non-bender. Pressure points, acrobatics and shutting down benders up close.

⚔️

Weapons Master

Non-bender. Martial weapons. Still in development

Finding Your Way

You'll never be lost for long.

Quest Compass

Every citizen gets one on login. It points to your next objective and re-aims after each conversation or key kill.

Objective Line

Your current task shows above your hotbar with live counts, like Kill the Spider-Rats (2/3).

NPC Markers

A floating ! means someone has a new task for you. A ? means it's time to report back.

City Rules of Play

Bend Freely, Build Nothing

You can bend anywhere in the city, but you can't place or break blocks. Doors and trapdoors still work.

Quest Items Are Safe

Quest items can't be dropped. If you die, they move to your /backpack instead of falling on the ground.

No Bloodbending

Bloodbending is outlawed in 130 AG. It never unlocks, at any tier.
