Immobilize

Immobilize
Path
Chi Blocking
Tier
Tier III (250 QP)
Type
Combo

Lock a target in place for several seconds.

How to Use

  • Quick Strike, Swift Kick, Quick Strike, Quick Strike.

A combo: you perform it by chaining the moves above. /b help Immobilize shows the sequence in game.

How to Get It

Taught by Ty Lee's quests once you reach Tier III (250 QP).
