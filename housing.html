Finishing Arc 1 earns the key to your own room at Unity Flats. Decorate it, store your things, and sleep in your bed to respawn there.

Contents
  1. Getting Home
  2. Decorating
  3. Guests and Roommates

Getting Home

  • Right-click your apartment key, or type /apartment, to go home. Do it again (or use any iron door inside) to step back outside Unity Flats.
  • You can't head home for 10 seconds after taking damage.

Decorating

You can build and place things inside your room. Walls, floor and ceiling stay as they are. Furniture is sold by Landlady Fe, out front of Unity Flats. Unity Flats is also a /wp waypoint once you've walked past it. Bending, PvP and mobs are switched off in the building.
