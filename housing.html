When you hand Registrar Huan the Spider-Rat Queen's fang at the end of Quest 4, he gives you the key to your own room at Unity Flats. Decorate it, store your things, and sleep in your bed to respawn there.

Getting Home

  • Right-click your apartment key, or type /apartment, to go home. Do it again (or use any iron door inside) to step back outside Unity Flats.
  • You can't head home for 10 seconds after taking damage.

Decorating

You can build and place things inside your room. Walls, floor and ceiling stay as they are. Furniture is sold by Landlady Fe, out front of Unity Flats. Unity Flats is also a /wp waypoint once you've walked past it. Bending, PvP and mobs are switched off in the building.

Home Comforts

  • Healing at home. While you're in your own room (or one where you're a roommate), you slowly heal and your hunger refills.
  • Rest. Right-click your bed for full hunger and a "Well rested" note, once every 5 minutes. Sleeping in it also sets your respawn.
  • Whetstone. Place one and right-click it to repair the item in your hand for 10 Yuans. It only works in your own home.

Furniture and Trophies

Landlady Fe sells 17 pieces in a 130 AG style: chairs and cushions, tea tables and a Pai Sho table, paper and stone lanterns, a fire brazier, a folding screen, rugs, a deco lamp, the whetstone and a weapon rack. Right-click the floor or a wall to place a piece; hit it once to pick it back up.

Two pieces can't be bought. You earn them:
