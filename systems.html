Order & Chaos

Like Raava and Vaatu, two forces pull at Republic City. Quest choices push you toward one or the other.

Two Separate Meters

Earning Order never costs you Chaos, and the other way around.

Earned Through Choices

Report the bribe or use it as leverage. Hand over the evidence or pocket it. Arc 1's big choices come in Part 3.

On Your Record

Your dossier shows which way you lean, with both totals.

Money & Housing

💴

Yuans

Everyday money. Earned from quests and from the gang members who carry it. Spent on food, gear and, later, housing.

📜

Quest Points

Your service record. Unlocks bending tiers and faction ranks. Can't be traded, sold or bought.

Your First Apartment

Finishing Arc 1 gets you the key to Unity Flats, Apt 3C. Thin walls, loud trolleys. The apartment itself opens soon.

Non-Pay-to-Win

Nothing that makes you stronger will ever be sold. If a store opens, it's cosmetics only.
