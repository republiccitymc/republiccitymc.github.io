Paths & Discipline

At Tier III, pick one path: Metal or Lava, Lightning or Combustion, Equalist or Kyoshi Warrior. It's permanent.

Your dossier shows it as your Discipline under your element, or Sand / Ice · Healing · Plant once Tier II opens them. Rare technique scrolls from bosses can teach one move early.

Money & Housing

YuansEveryday money. Earned from quests (580 across Arc 1) and from the wallets that gang members and police recruits sometimes drop (0 to 10 Yuans). Spent at TenTen (weapons), Raku (ramen), Old Man Shen (materials) and Jian's Tea House (open again after Quest 3). Shady Zhen buys Triad loot off you, no questions asked. Prices are in the Field Guide.
Quest PointsYour service record. Unlocks bending tiers and roleplay ranks. Can't be traded, sold or bought.
Your apartmentFinishing Arc 1 gets you a key to your own room at Unity Flats, yours for good. Right-click the key (or type /apartment) to go home, and again to step back outside Unity Flats. Decorate it, store your things, and sleep in your bed to respawn there. Bring friends with /apartment invite <name> (they can visit) or /apartment trust <name> (a roommate who can build). Furniture is sold by the Unity Flats landlord.
Non-pay-to-winNothing that makes you stronger will ever be sold. If a store opens, it's cosmetics only.
